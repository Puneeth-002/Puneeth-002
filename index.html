Services

What I Edit

Focused categories where I apply my editing process to help creators and individuals turn raw recordings into clean, watchable stories.

Social Videos

Tailored edits for Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter feeds that communicate clearly without fluff or audio clipping.

  • Platform aspect ratios (1:1, 4:5, 16:9)
  • Clean title cards & lower thirds
  • Music ducking under dialogue

Short-Form Content

Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts built around quick pacing, attention-grabbing opening hooks, and kinetic captions.

  • Tight jump cuts & pause removal
  • Synchronized dynamic subtitles
  • Punchy audio accents & SFX

Vlogs & Storytelling

Longer-form video edits that give lifestyle, travel, and personal stories breathing room, structure, and emotional pacing.

  • A-roll narrative trimming
  • Complementary B-roll sequencing
  • Atmospheric soundtrack transitions

Event Videos

Memorable recaps for celebrations, gatherings, and ceremonies that preserve key moments with natural color and steady timing.

  • Key moment selection & highlights
  • Music beat synchronization
  • Color balance & exposure matching

About

Building the Craft

An honest perspective on where I stand, how I work, and the kinds of projects I’m eager to take on.

Hi, I’m Puneeth s gowda. I’m a freelance video editor based with a strong dedication to visual pacing, clean audio, and story structure.

“I’m not claiming to be a seasoned industry veteran yet — I’m actively sharpening my editing skills with every cut, project, and challenge.”

What drives me is the invisible craft of editing: how a well-timed cut keeps the viewer hooked, how audio beds create mood, and how thoughtful rhythm transforms ordinary footage into something compelling.

I am open and welcoming to suitable freelance projects — whether you are a creator needing consistent short-form edits, a YouTuber looking for help trimming raw footage, or someone wanting a clean event montage. I treat every project with thorough care, honest communication, and prompt revisions.
