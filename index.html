Director’s Log // About

Building the Craft

“I’m building my craft one thoughtful cut at a time.”

Hi, I’m Puneeth s gowda. I’m an independent freelance video editor with a strong appreciation for the invisible rhythm of editing — the subtle timing of cuts, the emotional weight of audio tracks, and the discipline to strip away what doesn't serve the story.

I am honest about where I am in my journey: I’m not claiming to be a 20-year veteran or an award-winning studio director. I am actively sharpening my editing skills with every cut, project, and challenge, and I bring thorough attention, timely communication, and genuine care to every piece of footage entrusted to me.

I am open and welcoming to suitable freelance projects — whether you need regular short-form reels for your social channels, help trimming creator vlogs, or an energetic event montage.
